We want

to save Split!

The hour is dire! Due to seasonal tourism and real estate speculation, renting an apartment in Split has become practically impossible... That's where you come in! As a proud envoy of the city, find the cheapest houses in town and then rent them at a low price, so that people have somewhere to live instead of the city becoming a ghost town for most of the year. Use your wits and strength in ths exciting adventure where even the quest for the greater good won't stop you from visiting your lonely grandma.
